# ZT-201 Participant Guide (fictional)
- Take one tablet daily at the same time, with or without food.
- Missed dose: if more than 4 hours late, skip it and take the next dose at the usual time. Never take two doses together.
- Vomiting: if you vomit within 30 minutes of a dose, do not take another tablet that day.
- Before the week-4 visit, do not eat for 8 hours. Water is fine.
- Alcohol: avoid alcohol for the duration of the study.
- Storage: keep tablets at room temperature, away from moisture.
